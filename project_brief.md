# My agent: FloraCare

One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant enthusiasts and greenhouse owners manage plant care, diagnose issues, and browse a plant catalog.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's home/greenhouse environment details (humidity, sunlight levels, pet safety needs) and historical care notes
- Tools: Plant care lookup, watering & fertilizing event logger
- Catalog/UI: Inventory catalog of active plants rendered as A2UI cards and schedule tables
- Image gen: Visual previews of mature plants, planter arrangements, and healthy leaf guides
- Sandbox: Calculates custom fertilizer dilution ratios, soil mix percentages, and watering schedules

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for fertilizer/soil calculations
